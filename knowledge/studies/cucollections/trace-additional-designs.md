Composition witness: grow a second dynamic-map segment, then insert keys7/9. Existing key7 in segment0 is retained; new9 may claim segment1. Wider nonpacked values must use split publication rather than pretending two64-bit words form an atomic packed slot. For concurrent multi-match retrieve, group matches into shared96-record buffer and atomically reserve global ranges before paired stores.

All major public containers are accounted for through concrete implementations above. Atomic memory visibility, wide-slot mixed operations, serialized format validity and probabilistic estimator accuracy remain explicit obligations rather than unvisited design families.
