# Simplex fields, encoding composition and level gating

A high-dimensional learned field may use simplex support (D+1 vertices) instead
of a Cartesian tensor-product stencil (2^D vertices). This changes the field's
representation and derivatives; it is not a semantics-preserving swap for an
arbitrary Cartesian field. Pinned source: [manifest](inputs/sources.json).

The 2D simplex witness is a support-level substitution, not a claimed seeded RNG
instance or native run. Its full vertices are(0,0,0),(-2,1,1),(-1,-1,2); their
weighted sum is(-.3,.1,.2). All three full vertices have zero coordinate sum.
Rounding/rank construction ensures adjacent regions meet under their declared
field contract; a complete reusable coordinate-construction rule is left open.

These groups are accounted for with named source mapping/adapter gaps. No new
checker is needed for the local Hessian identity or zero-factor witness. Existing
fragment serialization check covers network parameter layout, not simplex support,
LoD derivatives or concurrent child-stream writes.
