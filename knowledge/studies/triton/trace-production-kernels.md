## Composition and closure

Routed low-float expert matmul composes owner_routed_occurrence_copy@1 → runtime_work_descriptors@1 → grouped raster/persistent assignment → block_scaled_instruction_operands@1 or software decode → cyclic_tile_reduction_partition@1 when enabled → operator_fusion@3 after complete reduction → encoded_group_scale_recipe@1 plus a separately bound value/scale storage profile. These stages preserve occurrence, logical K, packed byte and scale-group identities separately. Source guards reject unsupported combinations instead of silently composing them.

Every materially distinct shipped NVIDIA production family above has a concrete source representative and an actual field/program binding or a new paired proposal. Remaining items are native numerical conversion, descriptor/consumer layout, proxy visibility, peer publication/progress and independent assurance in [outstanding checks](checks/outstanding.md), not unread production designs. [Executed witnesses](checks/results.md) cover the consequential finite mapping questions. No CUDA compilation, GPU measurement, multi-rank run, Store admission or commit was performed.
