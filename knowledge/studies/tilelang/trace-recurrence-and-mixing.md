# Chunked recurrence and token mixing

When a causal operator carries a compact state, ask whether token interactions
can be divided into a local triangular contraction and an incoming chunk state.
This differs from partitioning independent reduction terms: time order and the
state boundary are part of the invariant. Paths below are under the retained
tree's `examples/`; versions come from the starting pool unless linked as new.

The two new pairs contain the chunk expansion and block-inverse arguments.
Gate prefix scans, nonlinear delta-rule/WY derivation and complete Mamba state
propagation remain named algebra/producer gaps rather than being hidden under
generic reduction. Backward relatives reuse contraction ownership, reverse
sequence dependencies and saved-state/recomputation choices; this survey does
not claim a new proof of each algorithm's derivative or native execution.
