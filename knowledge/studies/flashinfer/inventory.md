The initial inventory comes from the public/module directories, JIT registration
directory, csrc/include device directories and root build files. Examples/tests
are caller evidence, not mandatory per-entry replay. Autotune/provider loaders,
logging, profiler and deployment infrastructure are not kernel transformations;
their contracts matter when they select or publish a studied device path.
Third-party submodule bodies are absent. CUTLASS remains user-paused and must
not be fetched. Root and KDA/per-file license notices remain with source.
