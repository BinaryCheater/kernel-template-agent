# Hyperconnection prenorm and Mega MHC

Pinned DeepGEMM paths are relative to [the retained source](inputs/sources.json). This trace covers the standalone SM90/SM100 prenorm APIs and both Normal/Shifted specializations of the SM100 Mega MHC API, including layout,scheduler,device mainloop and epilogue helpers. CUDA/source-selected instruction paths are distinguished from scalar checks; no device kernel was run.

## Standalone prenorm: two outputs from one A traversal

`tf32_hc_prenorm_gemm` accepts BF16 A `[M,K]`,FP32 B `[N,K]`,FP32 D and FP32 square sums. Without splits D is `[M,N]` and square sums `[M]`; with explicit S they are `[S,M,N]` and `[S,M]`. The kernel computes unnormalized GEMM partials and A-square-sum partials. It does not divide D by an RMS scale. The source test explicitly sums the split outputs afterward (`tests/test_hyperconnection.py:24–43`).

Both launchers fix tile M64/K64,N aligned16,and K divisible64. The grid flattens `(M block,K split)`. K blocks are distributed by quotient/remainder: split i starts at `i*floor(B/S)+min(i,B%S)` and has `floor(B/S)+(i<B%S)` blocks. A17-block/four-split witness gives lengths5/4/4/4 with exact coverage. That unequal partition is not silently bound to the seed reduction_partition@1's uniform group_size formula; its representation remains a gap when the old no-empty constraint cannot express the source ranges.

SM90 permits N8/16/24/32 at the host boundary. TMA loads BF16 A and TF32-mapped B. Math threads load A once into FP32 register fragments and accumulate its squares while feeding TF32 register-A/shared-B WGMMA K8. The square sums cover each row's original BF16 values; normal BF16 significands fit logical TF32 precision on widening. SM100 casts A through registers into two tensor-memory operand stages,computes square sums in the cast role,and uses TF32 tensor-A/shared-B UMMA K8 into a separate accumulator region. Empty-cast and operand-empty barriers protect different lifetimes. Both stage the completed FP32 output through a swizzled shared buffer into TMA stores; scalar square-sum stores guard the M tail.

The helper `runtime_utils.hpp:85–89` maps FP32 tensors with allow_tf32 to `CU_TENSOR_MAP_DATA_TYPE_TFLOAT32`. [CUDA's tensor-map datatype reference](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cuda-driver-api/cuda_driver_api/group__CUDA__TYPES.html) identifies that datatype,while the exact conversion/denormal/rounding behavior remains unbound in this study. `operand_precision_conversion@1` is a partial match with from_dtype=f32,to_dtype=tf32,site=on_load; no rounding value is guessed. Widening BF16 is an exact representation operation on the checked normal-significand domain,not a proof of hardware subnormal behavior.

Two admitted-host boundaries need further evidence. SM100's host permits N up to128,but its device requires `swizzle_cd_bytes/4==BLOCK_N`; the helper chooses a swizzle no larger than128. Substitution leaves only N8/16/24/32. N40 yields BLOCK_N48,swizzle64 and a false device static condition16==48. This is a source specialization counterexample; compilation was not executed. Also,the public split count can exceed K blocks. K64,S2 yields an empty second split; SM100 then has no initializing MMA before accumulator consumption. Keep empty-split device behavior open rather than claim it follows the nonempty path. Mega MHC explicitly excludes empty producer splits.

## Mega MHC contract and shared numerical boundary

Mega MHC is SM100-only with four routes,24 HC outputs,tile M64/K64 and hidden size divisible1024. The normal inputs are x `[T,H]`,residual `[T,4,H]`,post mix `[T,4,1]`,Comb `[T,4,4]`,Fn `[24,4H]`,scale/bias vectors and RMSNorm weights. Shifted additionally supplies previous Pre coefficients and an output for new Pre. Normal computes a new Pre for immediate consumption without exporting it. FP8 output and BF16 output are independently optional but at least one is required. FP8 chooses exactly GEMM SF alone or routed SF plus shared SF; shifted state is all-or-nothing (`csrc/apis/mega_mhc.hpp:119–218`).

For each token and hidden index,the Post stage forms output route j as `x*post[j] + sum_i residual[i]*Comb[i,j]` in fixed source order,then rounds it to BF16. Its column-oriented Comb index is `i*4+j`; an asymmetric route witness rejects a transpose. That rounded residual is written to global output,converted back to FP32 for HC GEMM operands,and used for the HC squared norm. It is also the input to Pre accumulation. Fusing these consumers cannot remove the BF16 round: the witness raw values `[257,513,769,1025]/256` round to `[1,2,3,4]`,changing both their GEMM contribution and their square sum. The square sum after rounding is30; before rounding it is different.

Shifted Pre uses the supplied previous coefficients to produce X1 and rounds X1 to BF16 before computing X1 square statistics and materializing it. Normal waits for the newly computed Pre,then reads rounded new_residual,combines its four routes in FP32,rounds the result to BF16 and derives the RMS statistic from that rounded value. In both modes,RMSNorm multiplies by its scalar and weight in the source order and rounds the normalized output to BF16. FP8 quantization subsequently consumes those BF16-rounded normalized values.

Shifted FP8-only therefore still allocates a BF16 scratch tail: the public BF16-output flag cannot remove this materialized numerical boundary. Normal retains its combined BF16 values in registers across its row statistic and normalization rather than materializing X1. This is a conditional producer-consumer fusion/re-read choice; `operator_fusion@1` partially describes the on-chip intermediate but not every source numerical policy or tensor-memory edge.

## Split selection,workspace and task geometry

Deterministic mode fixes16 splits. Otherwise the host bounds candidate splits by `min(64,max(16,num_sms/num_m_blocks))`,then chooses the fewest splits achieving the shortest possible longest producer task in whole K64 blocks. For H7168,one M block and num_sms132 this gives112 K blocks and56 two-block tasks. The replay checks256 source-policy domains against an independent minimum-count condition. This policy changes grouping and can change rounding; it is not a performance measurement.

Source scratch has three regions: GEMM partials `[Mblocks,S,64,24]`,HC square sums `[Mblocks,S,64]`,and X1 square sums with the same shape. T13/H1024/S16 allocates106496 bytes before any optional BF16 scratch tail. Normal still reserves the X1-stat region even when unused. The source uses one persistent CTA per configured SM and static stride `task=cta+iteration*num_sms` for producer tasks. Consumer token ownership remains modulo num_sms even as retired roles join a shared CTA-local task queue.

Normal tasks use `token=cta+ticket*num_sms`; each ticket is claimed once by a whole warpgroup. Shifted Norm tasks flatten `(token pair,hidden-pack partition)` and map a CTA-local ticket t to `cta*4+t%4+floor(t/4)*(num_sms*4)`. The shared queue can be consumed by initially available and retired Post warps without losing those four residue classes. Twenty-eight token/grid domains check exact ownership,including T137 and partial token pairs. Hidden pack partitions use floor endpoints and cover each token pair's packs exactly once; odd final pairs load a safe alias for the absent token and suppress its stores.

## Producer roles and the two completion domains

The768-thread CTA has six warpgroups. WG0's four warps load IO,load Fn,issue MMA,and perform output stores/coefficient prefetch. WG1/2 are Post workers. They interleave K8 atoms across a four-stage tensor-memory A ring while keeping separate square-sum partials. WG3 drains the two accumulator stages into global scratch. WG4 handles Shifted Mix and WG5 starts Shifted Norm. In Normal,WG4/5 start cooperative Mix/Pre/Norm immediately and each producer group joins after finishing its static work. Shifted permits retired Post groups to join Norm.

IO and Fn rings advance per K64 block;coefficients advance once per split task and remain in registers while the coefficient slot is released. The last IO store-drain window may prefetch the next task's coefficients. The Post stage publishes A only after tensor-memory stores are fenced; the MMA issuer releases A/Fn stages only after their instruction uses. The workspace epilogue releases accumulator columns after loads to registers. Source register budgets have static checks,but transition liveness,allocation waits and resident-role progress still require a device protocol check. Existing warp_specialization@1's fixed-role/limited-group model is a partial match to this six-group role-retirement topology.

Mix and Norm are distinct completion domains per M block. Mix joins every split's GEMM and HC statistic. In Normal its split arrival additionally waits for new_residual global stores,so the fused consumer can re-read them. In Shifted,Mix need not wait for the separate Norm materialization. Shifted Norm joins only after X1 TMA stores and X1 square statistics complete. Releasing a local IO stage does not itself mean either global completion domain is ready.

The source reuses per-stream persistent split-barrier storage and warms it before CUDA Graph capture. `scheduler/mega_mhc.cuh` combines epoch e with count c as `(e+1)*128+c`. Producers wait for a current-epoch initialized count before release arrival; consumers acquire the exact terminal count. The last CTA to retire advances the shared epoch. [epoch_tagged_completion@1](delta/epoch_tagged_completion@1/template.yaml) owns this completion object,which does not perform numerical accumulation. Humming's first_writer_atomic_combine@1 and ordered_global_combine@1 instead update a shared numeric accumulator and do not express this join of separately stored payloads.

The wrapper `ptx/ld_st.cuh:350–351` emits `red.async.release.gpu.global.add.u64`,while acquiring waits use `ld.acquire.gpu.global.u64`. [NVIDIA PTX red.async](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/parallel-thread-execution/index.html#parallel-synchronization-and-communication-instructions-red-async) defines the asynchronous operation;source syntax alone does not prove the cross-CTA payload-release sequence. Scalar checks cover25 epoch/count domains,97 small publication orders and stale-tag/wrap counterexamples. Payload visibility,asynchronous completion and residency liveness remain separate obligations. A grid identifier is not used as the source's replay generation key.

## Mix nonlinear stages and finite row/column rescaling

After Mix completion,a token's warp reduces HC norm statistics across splits and combines each GEMM output in split-index order. It computes the HC RMS scale from the sum over4H residual values,then applies one of three affine scale/bias policies for Pre,Post or Comb. Pre uses sigmoid plus hc_pre_eps;Post uses sigmoid times hc_post_scale. Comb occupies lanes8..23 as a row-major4x4 matrix.

The first Comb stage is stable row softmax plus sinkhorn_eps,then column normalization by sum+eps. Later rounds normalize rows and columns by sum+eps. [row_column_rescaling@1](delta/row_column_rescaling@1/template.yaml) preserves this finite policy and requested iteration count. Positive epsilon and finite iterations do not imply exact unit row/column sums or convergence. An iterations1 call ends after the first column pass,not after a later row/column pair.

Rows are contiguous four-lane groups;column sums use XOR4 followed by XOR24 because the matrix is shifted to lanes8..23. Replacing XOR24 with a conventional XOR8 mixes inactive lane groups into the coefficients. An independent matrix interpreter and a source-layout interpreter agree over nine finite policies and reject that mutation,epsilon removal and a changed iteration count. The scalar check uses ordinary host exponential/division to check grouping/order;it does not certify CUDA __expf,fast_rcp or end-to-end tolerance.

## FP8 scale production and three output layouts

Each FP8 scale covers32 BF16 values;four scales occupy one int32. The helper chooses an exponent ceiling mapping amax into E4M3's finite range,with a minimum scale exponent-22. All32512 positive normal BF16 amax patterns satisfy the checked range/minimality equations. BF16 scaling and saturating FP8 conversion still require device numerical evidence. This is a producer of already-defined power-of-two scales;the existing power_of_two_scale_encoding@1 remains a value-preserving encoding step,not a substitute for the quantizer policy.

GEMM and routed SF differ by primary-writer strides: GEMM is MN-major,TMA-aligned; routed is contiguous token-major. Shared SF uses padded SF_BLOCK_M groups and an intra128-row permutation `(idx&31)*4+((idx>>5)&3)`. The source validates the backing storage capacity beyond the nominal `[T,H/128]` view. Thirty-six row-layout domains check injectivity and padding bounds,including SF_BLOCK_M224. Two byte-permutation steps pack four independently tagged SF bytes;tests retain their identities. Merely reshaping the routed or GEMM view does not construct this shared-expert layout.
